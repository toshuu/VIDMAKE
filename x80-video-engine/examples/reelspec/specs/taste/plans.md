# Taste-test plans (planning-dept §7.1 contract — written BEFORE any JSON)

## W01 — pin-tumbler lock (mechanism brief)

1. **Idea:** a lock opening, seen from inside the mechanism.
2. **Angle:** the lock picks itself — watch the exact moment each pin surrenders. (Not "how locks work" in general; the surrender moment.)
3. **Hook:** a brass key already sliding into frame in second one, pins trembling.
4. **Metaphor:** physical process — sectioned lock, shear line as the horizon everything must reach.
5. **Beats:** key enters → five stacks rise one-by-one (drivers park on the shear line; keys travel, drivers barely move — real mechanics) → flash + shackle pops + CLICK.
6. **Tone/energy:** `confident` — one claim per act, mono type, hard cut + slide (no fades anywhere in this reel).
7. **Sequential:** 5 key/driver pairs staggered (index × 6f); drivers land in cascade.
8. **Reading budget:** act1: 3 words; act2: 4 words; act3: 1 word. All far under floor.
9. **Restraint (3 decisions):** brass/steel/pin-red only · sectioned lock staging · staggered surrender.
10. **Tic check:** "FIVE PINS" is a count, not a hero-number flex — small mono, left edge, justified as content. Alignment mixed. Transitions slide/none. Chrome false. Dead space: body fills mid-frame; checked.

## W02 — storm umbrella (deadpan product trial)

1. **Idea:** an umbrella subjected to a wind trial it finds boring.
2. **Angle:** deadpan trial-by-combat — wind speeds as opponents, product unimpressed. The comedy is the flatness.
3. **Hook:** "RATED TO 120 KM/H." stated flat in second one while rain already hammers.
4. **Metaphor:** trial by combat — three rounds called like a fight (60/90/120), verdict without celebration.
5. **Beats:** claim under rain → three rounds arrive one-by-one → "STILL DRY." (period included).
6. **Tone/energy:** deadpan-`confident` hybrid — hard `none` cuts only, mono rounds, no whoosh-feeling motion.
7. **Sequential:** round labels stagger 12f apart; canopy wobbles on keyframes throughout.
8. **Reading budget:** act1: 7 words; act2: 6 words; act3: 2 words. Under floor.
9. **Restraint (3 decisions):** black/rain/yellow-signal only · perpetual rain bed · clipped-dome canopy motif repeated at two scales.
10. **Tic check:** "120 KM/H" numerals are the product's entire meaning (the rating IS the brief) — justified per §7.6. Left/center mixed deliberately (rounds left, verdict center). No fades. Chrome false.

## W03 — phantom jam (system science)

1. **Idea:** traffic jams with no cause, shown as a wave traveling backward through cars that were fine.
2. **Angle:** you ARE the wave — top-down, one brake light ignites, red walks rearward. (Not "traffic is bad"; the backward wave is the whole video.)
3. **Hook:** a single glowing brake dot igniting on an otherwise smooth road in second one.
4. **Metaphor:** system/simulation — dots + lanes + one red pulse; the road is a petri dish.
5. **Beats:** smooth flow (cars glide) → one driver taps out (ignition) → wave propagates rearward car-by-car → "NO CAUSE. JUST MATH."
6. **Tone/energy:** `clean` — comfortable holds so the wave reads; wipe-up + slide (no fades).
7. **Sequential:** brake glows stagger rearward (index × 10f, rightmost first — direction matters, documented).
8. **Reading budget:** act1: 5 words; act2: 5 words; act3: 4 words. Under floor.
9. **Restraint (3 decisions):** asphalt/white/red only · rounded-rect cars · one red pulse motif.
10. **Tic check:** zero numerals-as-heroes. Left type. No fades, no chrome. Dead space: road band is the stage; top/bottom void is intentional (checked — the void isolates the system).
