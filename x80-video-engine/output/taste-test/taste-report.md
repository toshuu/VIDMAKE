## What the instruments did

- `checkSpec` reading-budget fired 4×: **1 true positive** (w02-a1 last
  round label held 18f < 24f floor → stagger tightened 12→8, re-rendered
  clean) and **3 borderlines** (static full-act copy at 54–63f need vs 60f
  acts — reviewed, kept, justified in §7.6 terms).
- Calibrated once mid-test: lone punctuation tokens (—, ·) no longer count
  as words. Limitation logged: the rule is a *load alarm* (total words vs
  act), not a per-line settle proof — sequential arrivals can still trip it
  after per-line floors hold. Next refinement, not this pass.
- Screenshot critique caught **1 real failure**: w01-a3's 22% gold wash
  read as dirt and the shackle floated context-free → flash rebuilt as a
  3-frame white pop + lock body restored. Re-rendered, fixed. The loop works.

## Verdict

**Less formulaic on every measured axis.** The plan block forced premise
decisions that batch rounds never wrote down (angle/metaphor/hook as
separate artifacts), the banned-default list with written justifications
turned two would-be reflexes into conscious choices, and zero fades across
3 reels happened by contract, not taste-of-the-day. The remaining risk is
volume: 3 reels prove the workflow, not its durability — run the next batch
under §7 and watch whether the tic counts stay down.
